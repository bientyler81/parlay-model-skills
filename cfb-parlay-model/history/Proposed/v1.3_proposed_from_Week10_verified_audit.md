## Edit 1: Section 13D, Efficiency vs Scoreboard
- **Current:** "Efficiency vs. Scoreboard: Compare garbage-time-filtered EPA/play, success rate, and yards per play against the final margin. Identify who won the *process* versus who won the *scoreboard*."
- **Proposed:** keep that sentence and add: "A REGRESSION UP/DOWN tag requires EPA/play or post-game win expectancy (e.g. GameOnPaper Deserved Win %) to agree in direction with the yardage gap. Total yards and first downs alone never support a regression tag."
- **Evidence:** In Week 10, LSU outgained Alabama 457 to 285, but Alabama had the better EPA/play (+0.15 vs -0.14) and a 99% Deserved Win %. Texas A&M outgained South Carolina 457 to 360, but EPA/play was about even, A&M's Deserved Win % was 59, and A&M had +10 points of turnover luck.
- **Sample-size check:** a structural rule about which evidence counts, not a reaction to one game's result.
