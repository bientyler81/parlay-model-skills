# Proposed edits for v1.4 (from the Week 11 audit, Nov 17 2026)

Status: AWAITING THE USER (Approve / Reject / Modify). Evidence: `08_Weekly_Reviews/Week11_Audit.md`.

## Edit 1: Section 9, QB status on official reports
- **Current:** "For SEC & Big Ten games: State exact status from the official conference availability report (Out, Doubtful, Questionable, Game-Time Decision)."
- **Proposed:** add "A starting QB listed Probable, Questionable or Game-Time Decision, or a QB whose coach has not named a starter, is **UNCERTAIN** until confirmed by the game-day report or warmups. Any leg that depends on that QB carries Low confidence and must be re-checked at the final report; if the ticket locks before then, say so in the lock-timing item."
- **Evidence:** Week 11. LaNorris Sellers was Probable on the SEC Friday report and did not play (SC lost 34-6 as a 6.5-point favorite). Malik Washington (GTD) sat, and backup Cardell Williams went 21/23 in a Maryland upset. Josh Hoover was not on the report, but Grant Wilson started. That's 3 of 7 tracked QB situations.
- **Sample-size check:** structural (report labels are self-reported and not binding); not a reaction to a single result.

## Edit 2: Section 5, travel disruption is not a named adjustment
- **Current:** adjustments allowed for "...significant motivation/rest asymmetry."
- **Proposed:** add "Travel disruption by itself (late arrival, same-day flight, bus trip, changed kickoff) is **not** a named reason to move probabilities off the market. The market prices it within hours. Adjust only for confirmed consequences, such as a player ruled out."
- **Evidence:** after Friday's FAA ground stop, the 11 travel-disrupted road teams went **6-5 ATS**, while the market had already moved 1-2 points against them. The one leg where I added travel credit (UCF ML, +1.1 vs ASU's same-day flight) lost 26-13; ASU deserved 97%.
- **Sample-size check:** 11 games in one week, and it agrees with the efficient-market default in Section 1. Structural.

## Edit 3: Section 11, PrizePicks Team Picks pricing check
- **Proposed:** add to Section 11:
  > "PRIZEPICKS TEAM PICKS: before the parlay verdict, record the app's multiplier for each pick and the ticket total (the user's report or screenshot). Compute each pick's app ÷ fair (fair = 1 / vig-free market probability) and the ticket's app ÷ fair. Because the margin applies per pick, prefer 2-3 picks. A ticket paying under 90% of fair is a PASS unless the model's combined edge (Π model p ÷ Π market p) is at least fair ÷ app. Flag any single pick paying 100%+ of fair as a value pick."
- **Evidence:** Week 11 ticket quotes were CT2 79%, CT3 82.5%, CT4 78% and CT5 87% (model) of fair. Per pick: 91% to about 100% (LSU). The model's combined edge on 4-pick tickets was about +8%, far short of the 15-25% gap.
- **Sample-size check:** structural (the pricing math), backed by 4 ticket quotes and 6 per-pick quotes.

## Edit 4: Section 7 4th-down flag must pass the Section 13D test
- **Current (Section 7):** "Flag any team that lost by 10+ despite winning total yards/YPP due to ≥ 3 failed 4th-down attempts as a prime REGRESSION UP candidate."
- **Proposed:** append "...only if EPA/play or post-game win expectancy (Deserved Win %) also favors that team (Section 13D). Yardage alone never earns the tag."
- **Evidence:** Oklahoma State lost 52-27 to Texas Tech, won total yards 465-442 and failed 3 of 4 on 4th down. By the Section 7 rule it's a REGRESSION UP candidate. But its EPA was -0.09 vs Texas Tech's +0.21, and its deserved win % was 3%.
- **Sample-size check:** makes Section 7 consistent with the v1.3 13D rule; no new behavior.
