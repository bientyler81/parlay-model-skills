## Edit 1: Section 7, a Pythagorean flag alone is context, not a named reason
- **Current (Section 7):** "Pythagorean Expectation: PF^2.37 / (PF^2.37 + PA^2.37) vs. actual win %. If actual win % deviates by >= 0.18, flag REGRESSION DOWN or REGRESSION UP."
- **Proposed:** append: "A Pythagorean flag by itself is **context, not a named reason** (Section 5): it cannot move a probability, lower a grade, or drop a leg unless the team's garbage-time-filtered EPA margin (CFBD) also says its record is ahead of (DOWN) or behind (UP) its play. Without that agreement, print the flag and lower data confidence one step only."
- **Evidence:**
  - Week 12: Pythag flags went **2-5 ATS**. Right: Nebraska (UP; beat Ohio State), Kentucky (DOWN; lost by 14 at Missouri). Wrong: Utah (DOWN; won by 21, deserved 99%), Stanford (DOWN; won by 16), LSU (UP; lost by 14), Clemson (DOWN; won by 10 at Duke as an 11.5-point dog), Arizona State (DOWN; covered).
  - Three Week 12 grades were moved by a Pythag flag alone, and all three moved the wrong way: Utah ML dropped (would have hit), Stanford ML cut B- to C+ (hit), Tennessee ML cut C+ to C on LSU's UP flag (hit).
  - Week 11: 3-2 (Kentucky, Clemson, Nebraska right; Stanford, ASU wrong). **Two weeks: 5-7.**
- **Sample-size check:** 12 flags over two weeks, which is small, but the edit is structural. Section 5 lists the named reasons (line, post-line injury news, wind, motivation/rest), and a season's points for and against is public information the market already prices. The flag was being used as a named reason without being one. The same agreement test is already required for regression tags in Section 13D (v1.3) and for the 4th-down flag in Section 7 (v1.4).

## Edit 2: Section 10, a conditional Keep becomes Drop if the condition is unresolved at lock
- **Current:** per-leg tag is "Keep, Replace, or Drop." Week 12 legs carried "Keep only after IU names a starter," "Keep only after the ILL QB is known," and "Keep only if wind holds," with no rule for what happens if the condition is still open when the ticket locks.
- **Proposed:** add to Section 10: "A conditional Keep ('Keep only after X') must name its condition and the report that settles it. If X is not confirmed on an opened page before the ticket's lock, the leg is **Drop** for that ticket. Expected or likely is not confirmed."
- **Evidence:** Indiana ML (B, Keep only after IU names a starter). Indiana never named a starter before kickoff ("Wilson expected, not confirmed" at the Saturday 11:25 AM ET re-check). Cignetti benched Wilson for Hoover in the third quarter, and Indiana lost 27-24 in OT (Washington deserved 55%; EPA Washington +0.16 vs +0.13). Iowa ML (Keep only after the ILL QB is known) was settled by the gameday report (Houser played) and hit.
- **Sample-size check:** 2 conditional legs this week, so this is not a results-based edit. It closes a gap in the v1.4 Section 9 QB-uncertainty rule, which says to re-check at the final report but does not say what to do when that report never settles it.
